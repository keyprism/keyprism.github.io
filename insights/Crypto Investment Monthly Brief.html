Executive Summary

Market Contraction Signals Consolidation Phase. April 2026 marked a significant inflection point: total funding plummeted 74–75% MoM to $640–839M across 48 deals—the lowest monthly total since July 2024. This deceleration reflects broader market caution despite Bitcoin's +12.07% monthly gain, its strongest performance of 2026.
